# If $250k is invested tomorrow

Plano condicional, não oferta recebida. Investimento/regras do accelerator dependem de seleção e diligência; [programa oficial](https://colosseum.com/worldsfair).

## Budget envelope — ASSUMPTION

| Allocation | USD | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder runway + focused engineering (contract/hire) | 110,000 | Prototype hardening, SDK integration, device clients |
| Independent security assessment/audit | 35,000 | Threat review, contract/protocol audit, remediation |
| Legal/regulatory + partner structuring | 25,000 | Roles, custody/liability, licensed PSP agreement |
| Pilot operations / merchant training | 25,000 | Consented pilot delivery + measured outages/economics |
| Infrastructure / testing / monitoring | 15,000 | Reproducible staging, telemetry and incident processes |
| Contingency | 40,000 | Delay/security remediation; no automatic loss guarantee |
| Total | 250,000 | No spending authorized by this document |

## 0–3 months

Founder + one senior engineer/contractor; interview merchants and operators; resolve liability and funding partners; independent design audit; no real-money pilot before approvals. TARGET: 15 merchant/operator interviews, two written pilot scopes, zero unresolved critical security issues, one PSP technical feasibility response.

## 3–6 months

One segment, one geography, small caps, rollout contingent on audit/counsel/partner. TARGET: 10 active merchants, first paid customer or documented rejection, ≥95% eligible intents settled within agreed window and losses inside contractual budget. Zero observed losses in small n is not zero risk.

## 6–12 months

Integrate a second POS/organizer, measure recurring adoption and fully loaded margin. TARGET: 100 recurring merchants, positive contribution margin, two renewal decisions and setup repeatable without founder at every merchant. Stop acquisition if loss or support burden breaks economics.

A new chain only after buyer/cost requirement; not as default hiring task. Monthly runway and milestone review determines further hiring. These are targets to test, not projections promised to investors.
