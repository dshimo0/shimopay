## Patterns with defensible evidence

Several descriptions identify a buyer/job in one sentence; use that clarity. Prize recipients span assets, workflows and middleware; no single technical pattern guarantees winning. Being named in portfolio is evidence of Colosseum involvement, not revenue, retention or independent funding. These summaries do not justify claims that demo quality caused an award.

## Next evidence needed

Copilot full records/demos/repos for each comparable; inspect available pitch videos and primary current sites. Score actual demo clarity, depth, model, distribution and founder fit only afterward. Do not replace missing cells with invented grades to satisfy the table.
